Aklanon-English Dictionary

    About this dictionary

    This app holds a selected set of entries from A Study of the Aklanon Dialect, Volume Two: Dictionary (of Root Words and Derivations), Aklanon to English by Salas Reyes, Zorc, and Prado (1969). New entries can be added to the word list without changing the app.

    Reading an entry

    • An acute accent marks the stressed vowel. túbo (pipe) and tubó (sugar cane) are different words.
    • An apostrophe marks a final glottal stop, as in bága' (lungs).
    • (h) marks a final /h/ that appears when the root takes an ending, for example tinda(h) becomes tindahan.
    • Search a word with a prefix, such as paabá, and the app shows the root it comes from.

    Word kinds

    • Teal edge: verbs. RV1 to RV9 are the source's classes of regular verbs (pages 25 and 26 of the source). CV is a causative verb.
    • Gold edge: nouns.
    • Grey edge: adjectives, interjections, particles, and letters.

    Entries were transcribed from a scanned copy and meanings follow the source. If you find a mistake, tell the researcher so it can be corrected. After your first visit the app works without a connection.
